# AI usage log

- AI assistance was used to help structure the project, draft Python analysis/dashboard code, generate documentation and prepare a business memo.
- The source dataset was inspected with Python/pandas. Status values were normalized and non-final statuses were excluded from the resolved-order RTO-rate denominator.
- A transparent rule-based risk score was implemented and documented. It is not presented as a trained machine-learning model.
- AI-generated business recommendations and WhatsApp templates are proposals for human review. No external WhatsApp API was called and no customer was contacted.
- All calculated metrics should be independently reviewed before business use. Scenario estimates are assumption-based and are not guaranteed savings.
